## Distinct visible motif sets

The October videos clearly show a fleet. The following are **separate visual candidates**, not one combined livery:

1. **Flower-band hull:** `fneO2kuutM0-0200.jpg` is an excellent long side view. Dark green upper ground with repeating pink/red lotus-like flowers, yellow/gold flame petals and turquoise/cyan oval centers. Large and small flowers alternate. Lime rail and lime horizontal divider. Lower ground deep red/maroon with repeated yellow hooked leaf/curl units. At 250s a near-end deck cap has a large pink lotus with black outlines and lime edge. This matches the gross flower-band motif of the previously inspected Trà Tim October launch carried hull at 800s, rather than the separate ornate shelter hull.

2. **Diagonal-scroll hull:** `ufJGst1M7zI-0300.jpg`, `fneO2kuutM0-0300.jpg` and `fneO2kuutM0-0650.jpg`. Deep red/maroon fields with large gold/yellow scroll shaded orange/brown, some lime highlights; sweeping diagonal pink-to-pale bands plus a dark blue band. Narrow green strips contain regular gold/lime dots, bounded yellow. Adjacent lower sections contain repeated gold fan/lotus/scale-shaped units on darker ground. The layout consists of sweeping separated panels, not a uniform scale skin. Several similar panels/boats coexist, so whole-hull identity is not settled by one close crop.

3. **Red figurative end with gold eye and plaque:** `ufJGst1M7zI-0550.jpg` shows a red rising end with a prominent sculpted/painted gold eye/ear shape and a painted green/gold kneeling figure. At 650s another visible rising end carries an elaborate gold/orange Phoenix-like bird and an oval/rounded **blue plaque with white Latin text “CHÙA CHRÔITUMCHẮS”**, surrounded by gold/red scroll. A red fabric ceremony cover hides much of its tip. The plaque binds the monastery, not a race number. Do not infer that 550s and 650s are opposite ends of one boat without continuity evidence.

4. **Orange/yellow gradient high end:** `fneO2kuutM0-0550.jpg` shows a foreground rising end with warm orange-to-yellow ground, a gold/red scroll edge and large gold Phoenix-like feathers/scroll near its base. Behind it are two separate red rising ends with bright green/yellow leaf bands. This frame proves physical separation of boats; its foreground is not the same red end as every background boat.

## Dugout versus planked: supported distinctions and limits

The two October source titles distinguish độc mộc (dugout) from ghép / Phoenix. Actual frames show different boats side by side, different raised-end shapes and distinct motif sets. The dugout-title video at 450s shows red interior thwarts/ribs and a dark lower cavity; the Phoenix-title video at 200s shows a broad smooth painted side. Neither finished painted view independently reveals enough timber joinery to certify a one-log shell versus a planked shell. Added thwarts and ribs do not prove a planked hull, and an unbroken paint layer does not prove a dugout.

The August 500s frame is useful construction evidence: exposed rough timber at an end and a white outer patch/primer band, with an artisan actively working. It cannot be used to claim a final color or conclusively classify all the nearby finished boats. Accordingly, manufacturing labels remain **source-authored labels**, while separate physical hulls and their visible motifs are directly verified. Precise motif-to-manufacturing-label pairing needs a continuous walkthrough or a visibly named single-hull construction sequence.

## Race 08 comparison

Directly re-inspected the existing quarter-final `qf-850s.jpg` from [the 2025 broadcast excerpt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=51z0WQzXXFY&t=850s). Race 08 has a blue rectangular/rounded name plaque surrounded by gold/pink ornament and a gold/black decorated near end. This is **compatible with the plaque/diagonal-scroll candidate**, and less like a simple flower-only strip. However, motion blur, spray and bodies conceal the exact characters and most side panels. No October land frame in this pass displays bib 08. The monastery plaque alone is insufficient to distinguish all its hulls.

Therefore the finished land palette and motif candidates are usable evidence, but the exact race-08 hull remains a **candidate match**, not a confirmed reconstruction. Do not merge the flower strip, gradient Phoenix bow and diagonal scroll into a single supposedly authentic boat. A race-day bank frame showing the 08 plate plus blue name plaque and several adjacent panels would resolve the remaining assignment.
