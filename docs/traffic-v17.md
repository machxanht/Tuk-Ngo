Authored procedural scooters (with adult helmeted riders), cars and minivans use no external art or paid assets. Scooters dominate the deterministic 192-vehicle population, distributed across two directions on each bank.

Wheels rotate by travelled distance/radius in the vehicle's local frame; riders have modeled bent legs, hands to handlebars, helmet and facial front.
