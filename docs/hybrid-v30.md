# V30: close the sleeved jersey shoulder

The stable tablet URL is unchanged: https://machxanht.github.io/Tuk-Ngo/assets/illustrated-v22/.

The final public V29 close-up revealed a real garment defect: the blue caller sleeve exposed an open tube behind the shoulder when the arm was raised. The base vest still had tank-top armholes and their complete binding loops. Independent circular cloth rings, fitted approximately to arm radii and borrowing nearby vertex weights, did not form a continuous shoulder with this vest. The inspected source binding bounds identify nodes 0/1 as armholes (entirely outside ±.11m) and the neckline/hem as separate central surfaces.

V30 uses a compact anatomical shoulder/upper-arm shell at a 4.5mm normal offset, copying the exact four body joint weights and original smooth normals. It closes the base armhole while overlapping beneath the vest. The armhole bindings hide only for sleeved palettes; neckline/hem and sleeveless kits retain their bindings. This removes the detached ring and open shoulder without changing body anatomy, cap, skin UV/texture, hands, foot supports or role motion. Tailoring remains an authored close-fitting garment approximation, not recovered branded uniforms. Source GLB V27 stays generic; these sleeves are runtime additions.

`check-sleeve-fit-v30.py` measures actual posed garment/body pairs for seated/standing rowers and caller/controller across 17 poses. Maximum rest/posed distance is approximately .00450005m and joint-weight difference is zero. Direct blue caller face/body, white-sleeved Trà Tim and the four-role pose captures were inspected. All eight palette/livery and blue-interior checks passed, as did the twelve-face UV/normals gate. Rig continuity/grip/feet and four playable UI layouts passed. Two first concurrent local runs reported transient Failed-to-fetch; a fresh single-page diagnostic had no failed requests, and serial final checks passed. No change to the loader or a claim of general network recovery was made from that transient event.

The merged actor is now 14,163 triangles, with 948,411 per whole-boat render and 314 draw calls. Headless desktop stayed near the existing 30fps cap; physical tablet performance is unmeasured. Updated body/face/festival clips capture the current canvas, so the gallery does not show the superseded tube. V29 crowd, 61-entry roster and their public checks remain valid; no new crowds, roster edits, game-rule changes or architecture changes are part of this fix.
