# Crowd V16 independent identity and continuous motion

Only `src/race/crowd.mjs` changes. Existing CC0 MakeHuman-derived V10 VAT assets, batching, near/far selection and exported API remain. Root owns placement and independent elapsed-time clock.

The previous `id % 16` profile and `id % 7` motion selection made demographic and activity stripes following placement order. V16 avalanches integer ID and centimetre X/Z coordinates into a seed, then uses a separately salted channel for profile, age, clothing style, hair, colour, activity, phase, speed, intensity, width, height, skin tone, facing angle and breathing phase. The colour channel overrides placement shirt colours with sixteen warm/neutral/blue/green regional clothing colours. Determinism permits repeatable QA; it is authored pseudorandom variation, not measured demographic data.

A synthetic 5,000-person regular grid had 5.82% identical adjacent profiles (independent uniform 16-profile expectation 6.25%), 5,000 unique phases, 3,592 young-adult, 962 older-adult and 446 adolescent styles. This validates removal of ID modulo stripes, not the actual scene's final nearest-neighbour distribution. Actual demographics and first 24 hashed identities are exposed through `group.userData.crowd` for root scene QA.

All six licensed baked activities move: maximum coordinate differences against frame zero across 24 frames were cheer 0.28505 m, standing/seated clap 0.20155 m, wave 0.17691 m, seated watcher 0.01069 m and standing watcher 0.02245 m. Speeds, phases and amplitude blends use separate channels. Each instance additionally has continuous 5 mm upper-body breathing and 6 mm slow weight shift, driven by crowd elapsed time rather than boat play state. These subtle offsets preserve planted foot geometry. Two shared instanced LOD meshes per 80 m section remain; no individual skeletons or new textures are introduced. The extra two-float motion attribute adds eight bytes per instance per LOD.

Gender labels mean masculine/feminine styling on the existing single anatomical topology. They do not claim a newly modelled female body. Young/older styling varies stature and grey hair; adolescent styling scales height to 76% with existing head proportions. Small children are deliberately not claimed because a convincing child anatomy asset is absent. Clothing/headwear are visual styling, not portraits of real spectators.

Checks: `node --check src/race/crowd.mjs`, `npm run lint`, direct construction/disposal of a 5,000-instance crowd using actual near/far assets, independent-salt population check and all-frame VAT motion check passed. Browser visual acceptance and final scene neighbour counts remain root's integration gate; these structural checks alone do not prove visible age/gender realism or physical tablet performance.
