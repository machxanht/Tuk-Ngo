# Men's final eight: Maspéro / Sóc Trăng, Cần Thơ, 2025

Research date: 2026-10-11. Scope: actual 2025 team identities and result status; no implementation changes. “Top eight” means the eight men's quarter-finalists, not eight individually ranked places. This is the 1,200m men's event on 4–5 November 2025 at Maspéro, not the concurrent An Giang/Gò Quao event or an earlier Sóc Trăng season.
