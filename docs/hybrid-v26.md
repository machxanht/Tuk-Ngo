# V26: playable hybrid race and festival motion

The primary URL remains `https://machxanht.github.io/Tuk-Ngo/assets/illustrated-v22/`; root routing remains unchanged. V26 extends the existing hybrid renderer rather than creating another game or changing the pending legacy V21 work.

`src/race/gameplay.mjs` remains the single tested rules model. `hybrid-game-v26.mjs` exposes it to the standalone preview; `quick-race-v26.js` adapts state into the existing art projection and UI. Player progress, speed, cadence, stamina, bow crossing and opponent progress are live model values. The opponent is independent of player position. Rendering uses continuous bone cycles and the same depth-correct hull/footboards. Rendered distance is compressed to the existing illustrative course; the underlying game uses 1,200 m.

Three difficulties, a three-second start, a timing bar, one large stroke button, recovery/steady/sprint, pause/resume, visibility pause, interpolated finishes, win/loss/tie/DNF, replay and browser-local winning records are wired. Records have an in-memory fallback if storage is blocked. Controls remain outside the canvas, below it in ordinary layouts and beside it on short landscape layouts; fullscreen retains the timing/stroke panel. The asset inspector is hidden during a player race. The optional low-power switch lowers the 3D render resolution and draw target from 30 to 20 fps. It is not a physical tablet benchmark.

The twelve head variants modify one anatomical mesh with modest head width/depth and skin tone differences; no distinct scanned identities are claimed. A shader changes only the rowers' jersey zone per boat; caller/controller role colours and bindings remain intact. The active near crew is green and its opponent amber, illustrative colours rather than verified team-specific kits. Water droplets follow actual paddle contacts and gain density/height with tempo and power. Crowd activity increases late in a race and at results, using the reviewed affine painted-person motion. Source artwork and all 1,949 crowd placements remain unchanged.

Optional audio reuses the existing CC0 concert crowd recording with gesture-based playback, pause on hidden/paused state and a user toggle. It is not a Khmer chant or tournament recording. Audio and renderer dependencies are local; no new account/service/fee was introduced.

Rebuild browser bundles with `node scripts/build-preview-v26.mjs`. The original V25 Blender rebuild/pack scripts remain source-compatible and preserve their original geometry; after using the V25 packer, rebuild the V26 runtime bundles. Package/publish the same illustrated folder with `scripts/publish-illustrated-v22.ps1`.

Verification scripts: `check-gameplay.mjs` for the unchanged rules model, `check-hybrid-game-v26.py` for actual UI/touch/fullscreen/records and optional real-browser-clock race, `check-hybrid-kits-v26.py` for actual renderer jersey pixels, `check-hybrid-v25.py` for contacts/continuity, and the existing illustrated festival/automatic race regressions. Browser-clock play must be reported separately from accelerated model completion. Physical tablet, exact local architecture, unique sculpted faces, fully rigged crowd, historical kits and a multi-round tournament remain unverified or unfinished.

Final local evidence: four layouts (1024×768, 768×1024, 390×844, 834×480) passed actual input, duplicate prevention, pause, recovery, fullscreen panel separation, win/DNF, replay and persisted records with no page errors or failed requests. The automated actual-button browser-clock race pressed 275 times, won with 275 perfect strokes and a player finish of **190.6005 seconds**, taking **194.72 wall-clock seconds** including the countdown. This is not a human playtest. Headless rig rendering was 29.7 fps; palm gap remained 1.913 mm and ankle drift zero. Thirteen existing rules checks passed. Automatic-course/festival regressions passed three layouts. Colour pixel checks and crowd source/finite-transform checks passed. Reports are in `docs/qa/hybrid-v26/`.

`athlete-continuous-v26.webm` and `festival-continuous-v26.webm` are six/eight-second recordings of the actual current canvas. The original CC0 crowd MP3 and attribution are copied unchanged into the self-contained preview folder, avoiding reliance on a sibling folder in local preview servers. Actual media playback/clock progression and pause are checked alongside its sound toggle.
