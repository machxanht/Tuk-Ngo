All selected frames and source metadata are cached outside the repository at `E:/AI/Um Tuk/asset-studio/top8-2025-references/`. A timestamp identifies an inspected video frame. Upload dates were retrieved from YouTube metadata; they do not prove the exact filming day. Colors are visual descriptions, not measured paint values. Launch/training clothing is recorded separately from tournament clothing.

## Verification and remaining work

Selected land frames above were directly opened and inspected, including full-resolution bow/side details. These are real footage captures rather than thumbnails. Finished, readable land paint exists for named 2025 Trà Cuôn, Prêk On Đơk and Pôthi Khsach sources; other rows have useful partial or multiple-boat evidence. None of the ambiguous fleet candidates should be described as eight exact tournament-hull reproductions. Required next checks are distinctive bow/side matching to the race footage, especially Kos Thum 1, Tum Núp 2 and Trà Tim 08; unobstructed full-side evidence for Om Pou Year and final Satharam is also incomplete. No game asset, code, publication or commit was changed by this research.
