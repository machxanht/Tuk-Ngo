# Hai sân Campuchia: tư liệu và luật trong game

Rà soát ngày 24/10/2026. Đã bổ sung **Phnom Penh – Tonle Sap (1.700 m)** và **Siem Reap – sông đô thị (500 m)**. Hai cự ly này dựa trên tư liệu lịch sử dẫn ở dưới; chưa có đủ căn cứ để coi là cự ly và điều lệ chính thức năm 2026.

- **Phnom Penh:** đường trường, dòng ngang mạnh hơn, hao thể lực nhiều hơn và hồi sức chậm hơn. Bối cảnh gồm bờ hoàng cung, mái nhiều tầng, đình đón lễ, phố ven sông và đường chân trời bờ đối diện.
- **Siem Reap:** chặng ngắn, hành lang lái hẹp, tăng tốc ít tốn sức hơn. Bối cảnh là sông trong phố, đình ven sông, cầu ngoài đoạn đua, hàng quán và cây xanh; không đặt Angkor Wat cạnh sông.
- Cả hai có luyện tập, đua đôi với AI và cúp mời 8 đội. Về đích tính theo mũi ghe. Hòa thì đua lại; các lượt khác trong nhánh đấu được mô phỏng. Đây là luật game, chưa phải điều lệ chính thức Campuchia.
- Danh sách tách theo sân: **Maspéro 61 đăng ký, Phnom Penh 16 tên đội, Siem Reap 8 tên đội**. Phnom Penh dùng tư liệu 2019/2024; Siem Reap dùng kết quả giải sông đô thị 2018. Chữ Khmer và nguồn từng tên ở [CSV](cambodia-teams.csv). Mẫu ghe, số VĐV hiển thị và màu áo hiện dùng bộ asset chung; chưa phải bản sao của từng đội thật. Một tên mới chỉ có chứng cứ giải cấp tỉnh và một đội chèo kiểu chev được giữ ngoài danh sách chơi.
- Số lượng được báo cáo ở Phnom Penh 2024 là **346 ghe thi đấu**; một báo cáo đăng ký ghi **348 ghe / 22.042 tay bơi**. Hai con số thuộc phạm vi báo cáo khác nhau. Siem Reap 2023 được báo cáo có **32 ghe đua**. Không gán các số này thành danh sách giải 2026.

## Tư liệu chi tiết và giới hạn xác minh

Reviewed 24 October 2026. These are playable festival interpretations, not a
claim to reproduce the official 2026 event or its complete regulations.

## Phnom Penh / ភ្នំពេញ

The national celebration races on the Tonle Sap in front of the Royal Palace.
The [National Radio report, 15 Nov 2024](https://rnk.gov.kh/article/43375)
confirms the location and three-day format. Reporting distinguishes paddle,
chev/oar and international classes; these must not be treated as one identical
boat class. [A boatbuilder interview, 12 Nov 2024](https://thmeythmey.com/detail/141902)
records 346 boats: 170 paddle, 45 chev, 131 international, including women.
The [CamboJA registration report](https://cambojanews.com/all-systems-in-place-for-annual-water-festival-boat-race-in-phnom-penh/)
instead reports 348 registrations and 22,042 rowers. These are different counts,
not evidence for inventing a definitive 2026 roster.

A historical 1,700 m race is described in [a 2015 boat-committee interview](https://khmercircle.blogspot.com/2015/11/lack-of-boat-races-leaves-villagers.html)
(archived Phnom Penh Post reporting). This is historical evidence, not a
current official course measurement. The game uses a 1,700 m endurance course.
Its current, endurance tuning and cup bracket are authored game rules.

For visual layout use the [Cambodian Embassy's 16 Nov 2024 photo series](https://www.embassyofcambodiadc.org/news1/a-series-of-photos-along-the-tonle-sap-river-in-front-of-the-royal-palace-on-the-3rd-day-of-the-royal-festival-of-boats-floating-boats-and-the-full-moon-festival-ok-ambokphnom-penh):
broad river, west-bank Royal Palace roofs and public promenade, pavilion,
distant east-bank skyline. Do not put Angkor Wat next to the course.

## Siem Reap / សៀមរាប

[First-hand 2024 photographs and field notes](https://helloangkor.com/in-pictures-the-water-festival-siem-reap-2024/)
show the narrow urban river, Pokambor Avenue pavilion, bridges near NR6 and
Tep Vong, planted banks, markets and dense crowds. The [provincial spokesperson
interview in 2023](https://www.cambodianess.com/article/boat-rides-and-cultural-events-are-set-for-the-water-festival-in-siem-reap)
records 32 race boats; tourist boat rides are a separate activity.

[The 2008 technical-committee interview](https://khmernz.blogspot.com/2008/11/off-to-races-in-siem-reap.html?m=0)
(archived Phnom Penh Post reporting) describes 500 m, crews of 22, men's and
women's divisions and four groups followed by tournament racing. The game uses
a 500 m short sprint, with a narrower steering corridor and faster response.
The exact modern course length and full official local entry list are not
verified. Do not label these old rules as the 2026 regulations.

The [2025 AKP report](https://vps.akp.gov.kh/post/detail/352616) records a friendship
race and activities along the river and Royal Residence Park. Eight local
identities come from the [2018 technical committee results reported by AMS](https://ams.com.kh/news/central/161417),
four men's boats and four women's boats. This is a historical selection,
not a complete or current Siem Reap entry list. Royal Gardens and a modest riverside pavilion are appropriate;
Angkor Wat on the riverbank is not.

## Team identity and rules limits

The accompanying sourced team records retain Khmer spellings from interviews.
Romanised names are display transliterations, not official Vietnamese names.
Boat colours are game liveries unless a source explicitly establishes them.
Chev boats use a different rowing mechanism, so they remain in research only
until that rig exists. The existing paddle-crew rigs and shirt are preserved.
No historical rank changes a player's boat speed.

25 sourced identities are exported in [cambodia-teams.csv](cambodia-teams.csv)
and `public/assets/data/cambodia-teams.json`, with Khmer spellings and source
URLs. Eleven have 2024 identity evidence; five retain their 2019 results evidence. Sdach Damrey Sa Phluk Khiev Meanchey
Baramey Wat Aranh Sakor has a [2026 provincial-government report](https://takeo.info.gov.kh/km/article/01a13e92-415c-767b-827c-4bf4c090200a)
quoting the boat's abbot after provincial races. This does not establish an
official 2026 Phnom Penh or Siem Reap city entry, so this identity is unassigned
and excluded from play. Sixteen identities are assigned to Phnom Penh;
eight additional 2018 city-event identities are assigned only to Siem Reap.
Event membership is explicit; a team's home province is not used to infer it.
The invitational cups use
eight equal game hulls; actual crew sizes, class divisions, uniforms and hulls
are not reproduced. The 46-rower Tepthida record remains research-only because
its reported chev mechanism needs a different rig.

| Venue | Evidence distance adopted | Game stages | Play difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maspéro | Existing 1,200 m game course | 0–200 / 200–950 / 950–1,200 m | Balanced two-button race |
| Phnom Penh | Historical 1,700 m | 0–200 / 200–1,450 / 1,450–1,700 m | More stamina drain, slower recovery, stronger cross-current |
| Siem Reap | Historical 500 m | 0–80 / 80–350 / 350–500 m | Short sprint, lower boost cost, narrower steering corridor |

All three support practice, AI heats and a game knockout cup (61 teams at
Maspéro; eight invitations at each Cambodian venue). Crossing order is timed
at fixed 240 Hz with interpolated bow crossings. Other bracket heats are
seeded simulations. UI and audio milestones use actual course distances;
the last 100 m and 50 m announcements never reuse 1,100/1,150 m as Cambodian
world coordinates. Currents, lane widths, stamina values and ties/replays are authored game balance, not measured hydrology or official rules.

Winning means the bow crosses first. No deliberate contact; each boat stays
inside its corridor. Countdown, ties/replays, exhaustion and elimination remain
explicit game rules. Published reports are not a complete rulebook; no invented
foul or tie rule is presented as an official Cambodian regulation.

## Streets and catalogue update

Phnom Penh now uses a wider Sisowath-style promenade, two car lanes per
 direction, kerbs, junctions and crossings, arcaded shophouses, a west-bank
 royal compound with gardens, and an inland east-bank skyline. Siem Reap uses
 lower tiled shophouses, Pokambor-side pavilion/market, a Royal Gardens /
 Residence interpretation and balustraded bridges outside the race. Both
 retain bright daylight. These are stylised interpretations, not surveyed
 replicas. The earlier invented Phnom Penh steel arch has been removed in the mapped-layout update below.

Visual references inspected: [2024 riverfront preparation photo](https://b2b-cambodia.com/news/cambodia-water-festival-2024-what-to-know/),
[Tourism Ministry riverfront description](https://tourismcambodia.org/public/provinces/search/detail/167/riverfront),
[Siem Reap festival photo and announcement](https://www.information.gov.kh/articles/142214),
and [first-hand Pokambor / bridge festival notes](https://helloangkor.com/in-pictures-the-water-festival-siem-reap-2024/).

At the user's requested traffic mix, cars/vans/minibuses are 80% of the
 simulated Cambodian traffic entries. This is an art-direction ratio, not
 a measured Cambodian transport statistic. The original Maspéro mix is
 preserved. The Cambodian default hull is the existing dugout art on both
 lanes; it does not establish that each named real boat is hewn from a single
 tree. Individual construction remains unverified where source evidence is absent.

The selector is shared with online/opponent selection and supports Khmer,
 accent-insensitive Latin and province search with bounded rendered rows.
 Each map's picker, opponent, cup, online queue and friend room are isolated
 to its own roster; neither Cambodian map shares the other's teams. Each
 invitation cup still draws eight. See [catalogue review](cambodia-roster-review.md) for the exact
 additions, historical years, 260-name photograph source and remaining 300+
 roster gap. Do not describe the full 300+ import as complete.

## Per-venue event identity and inland variety

`venue-identity.mjs` provides separate event names, Khmer place lines and palettes.
Phnom Penh uses royal navy/gold and the Tonlé Sap place line; Siem Reap uses
terracotta/teal and its city-river place line. Banner textures are shared within
each venue instead of generated once per pole. Both pavilions carry a larger
dedicated event panel; sponsor art remains the two requested fictional placements.
These are original game identities, not copies of official seals or a claim to
2026 tournament sponsorship.

The Cambodian inland area now mixes six light instanced building families:
shutter townhouse, raised timber, courtyard, flat roof, tiled veranda and colonial
balcony. Heights, shutters, verandas and roofs vary within bounded plots. Coconut
fronds use a small feathered mesh, and broadleaf crowns vary between trees. These
families extend the references above; their exact distribution and dimensions
remain authored scenery. Road/river access corridors are preserved.


## Mapped riverfront and field-photo rebuild

The user approved maps and field photos after local Google Maps browser control
was unavailable. Google Maps 3D was **not** inspected. The current scene now uses
[a compact ODbL dataset](../public/assets/maps/cambodia-layout.json), derived
from official OSM map extracts. The full [sources and limitations](../public/assets/maps/cambodia-sources.md)
ship with the game and are linked from its settings.

Phnom Penh separates the west-bank Royal Palace, Moonlight Pavilion, Throne Hall,
Silver Pagoda and National Museum. The opposite bank follows mapped transverse
river extents rather than mirroring a 180 m channel. In the playable region its
width varies approximately 360–520 m, after straightening. The 59 m Throne Hall
spire is supported by the Tourism Ministry; most other dimensions are estimates.
The pavilion uses an open colonnade and gold/green layered roofs based on a
verified front photograph. Palace auxiliary structures use mapped locations.

Siem Reap places the Royal Residence, Royal Gardens and shrine by NR6 near the
start, FCC Angkor downstream, and the Tep Vong bridge near the finish. The low
white/red residence replaces the pair of generic houses. The two bridges are
outside the game crossing lines. No Angkor Wat temple is placed on this bank.

The river is straightened to preserve existing boat dynamics. Longitudinal
coordinates are normalised to game length; Siem Reap is widened to a minimum
38 m paddle/steering corridor. The start/finish anchors and current festival
street closures have not been surveyed. Facades and incidental streets remain
stylised; this is an improvement in sourced layout, not a digital twin.

Cambodian national flags use the public-domain 3:2 SVG with a shared texture and
shader cloth animation. Landmark details are batched; the original shirt math,
eight master athlete rigs, rosters, audio and race rules are unchanged.

Reproduce the data extraction with `python scripts/build-cambodia-layout.py
--source-dir <folder-containing-the-two-official-map.xml-extracts>`. The generator
contains the exact feature IDs and projection assumptions. `test-cambodia-geography.mjs`
checks landmark ordering, both shores, camera framing and bridge clearance;
`check-cambodia-map.py` renders the actual game and verifies flag loading and
animation. Physical-tablet FPS and a numeric visual-match percentage remain
unverified.
