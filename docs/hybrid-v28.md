# V28: eight 2025 crews on the shared hybrid race

Stable game URL: https://machxanht.github.io/Tuk-Ngo/assets/illustrated-v22/

The current hybrid renderer now uses the existing source-linked eight-team catalog and authored SVG kbach. Two selectors below the picture choose the near/player crew and far/opponent crew. Each crew renders with its own hull texture, torso, cap, binding and shorts palette. Known caller/controller palettes are separate. Kos Thum and Satharam use mixed caps; Satharam includes occasional alternate orange tops, and Kế Sách uses its recorded green-to-yellow torso. The generic sewn base garments and caps remain approximations, not recovered sponsor uniforms. Khmer back lettering remains the owner's custom `កូនខ្មែរ`.

Original hull UVs are restored for SVG liveries. Hull geometry, internal footboards, eight seated pairs, seventeen standing pairs, caller/controller/three helmsmen and eight shared motion rigs remain unchanged. Short sleeves use six smooth rings fitted to the anatomical upper arm, with nearest anatomical vertices supplying their original four skin influences. They add 480 triangles per actor, for 14,223 total. Source smooth normals and the V27 skin UV/texture survive this change. The downloadable athlete prototype remains the generic V27 source; team-specific styling and sleeve additions currently belong to the runtime.

Only two selected SVGs load initially. Each of the eight textures is cached after first selection. A texture failure keeps the previous pair and supports retry. Selections and the start button lock while loading; selection locks immediately during countdown, racing and pause. Team choice changes appearance only, with no invented real-team performance ratings. Existing difficulty-only local records, rules and AI behavior are preserved.

Sources are the preserved research in `top-eight-2025-results.md`, `top-eight-2025-appearance.md`, `chanhung-finished-kbach-catalog.md` and the follow-up hull documents. The new runtime copies original SVG bytes, not broadcast or photo pixels. Attribution travels with the copies in `public/assets/illustrated-v22/teams-2025/ATTRIBUTION.md`. These locate real appearance evidence, while painted banks remain approved style artwork.

The catalog's four leading ranks and four unranked quarter-finalists are preserved; no fifth-to-eighth ranks are invented. Individual boat dimensions, some land-hull-to-race-bib matches, hidden kbach, exact tailoring, sponsor print and cap ratios remain unresolved. Appearance notes distinguish later-uploaded recordings of the 2025 race from footage recorded in other years.

Build with `node scripts/build-preview-v26.mjs`. The existing publisher now copies preview subfolders, so the standalone Pages version includes these SVGs without publishing unrelated pending legacy changes.
