The end features and central lime scroll recur across these sources. This is a much stronger finished 2025 team-hull reference than the original partly blocked launch fragment. It is a **probable shared hull feature mapping**, not a numbered race-23 confirmation. No readable 23 plate was found in these sampled frames; a different pagoda hull or a late paint change cannot be excluded solely by matching general colors.

The safest authored interpretation from visible evidence is: black central ground with dense fluorescent lime scrolling leaves; red rail; red central name rectangle; red/orange rising ends with gold/lime ornament and a large cyan/blue figurative insert at one end. Exact hidden lower side, name lettering, complete figurative anatomy and mirrored/opposite-side arrangement remain unknown. The exact Khmer name should not be transcribed or generated from blurred marks.

Training clothing in the new sources is golden-yellow/salmon-orange sleeveless with blue/purple neck/arm and side trim, mixed caps and some black undersleeves. This does not replace the established race-day orange/red shirt and red-cap observations.

## Pôthi Satharam / A7: clearer finished border, full middle still unresolved

[Kim Bùi TV 2's longer launch ceremony](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8GN4hkDfqHY&t=120s), uploaded 2025-10-30, is titled for Pôthisatharam A7 Sóc Vồ, Sóc Trăng preparing for Maspéro 2025. Inspected `satharam2-0020.jpg`, `-0120.jpg`, `-0300.jpg`, `-0500.jpg`, `-0700.jpg` show the same finished red rising bow as the previous November-1 short upload. A7 is printed on shirts; caps visibly read CHÙA POTHISATHARAM. This provides direct team binding and another 2025 source.

At **120s/300s**, the previously obstructed lower bow band is clearer: **repeated yellow/gold curling wave/leaf units on dark red/maroon, between two yellow boundary lines**. Above it, a broad largely plain red field; an upper repeated yellow glyph-like band with dark outlines and a yellow baseline follows the rail. These repeating marks should not be claimed as a readable team-name transcription. The raised end includes a red sculpted animal head/eye, long red ribbons and two black tassels; the hanging front textile has circular colored mirror-like inserts. Those ceremony decorations may be removed for racing.

This new evidence supports a specific **lower repeating curl border** near the bow. It does not justify filling the entire middle with invented elaborate floral kbach. People and perspective still block most of the finished side. The longer upload remains inside the ceremony for the sampled points; its title alone cannot prove an unobstructed full broadside.

[Qui lam's joint Trà Tim / Satharam training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J4h2f_Zal-4&t=180s), uploaded 2025-10-26, was inspected at 60s/180s/350s/550s (`satharam-maspero-*.jpg`). Two boats have yellow and red-clad crews; both are distant or partly overlapped. At 180s the red-clad boat's near rising end has a red/yellow striped border consistent in gross palette with A7, but there is no readable boat number or name and no sufficiently clear middle ornament. **Do not assign either boat from crew color alone.**

The strongest numbered-race comparison remains the existing official 11,590s frame (bib 07, mixed red/orange kit, dark sleeve panels and orange/green caps), where the rising red end is compatible with the ceremony hull. That compatibility is not a definitive exact-hull match: side motifs are too distant and spray-obscured.
