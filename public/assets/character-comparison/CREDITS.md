# D/F athlete comparison

Original D character: **Quaternius Universal Base Characters Standard**, Superhero Male Full Body. Replacement hairstyles (Simple Parted, Buzzed, Buns and Long) also come from that Standard pack. CC0.

Original F character, F shirt and both characters' shorts: **Snow Rig © Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org**, CC BY 4.0. Source: Snow v4.2, downloaded from the official Snow v4 page. The embedded `ReadMe.txt` links to CC BY 4.0.

- https://studio.blender.org/characters/snow/v4/
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations: compact body skeleton, original Snow facial poses baked to Smile/Effort/Blink morph targets, portable UDIM skin atlas, seven skin tones, fitted replacement hair, eight facial identity profiles per character, shared blue racing artwork, shorts cut from Snow pants and removal of covered skin. D's new shirt follows D's own geometry and skin weights. Both characters' shorts remain Snow adaptations and retain this credit. The common Tuk Ngo paddle uses body-specific palm/arm/finger solutions and nine baked body clips for the two sides and crew roles. The crew page mounts both characters on the existing canonical hull with shared skeleton/material caches.

D/F downloads are source/animation inspection models. The game athletes retain their original GLB bodies, connected shirts, caps, hair and eight motion rigs. The head-only athlete-faces.glb derives sixteen generic heads from D/F, with baked identity shapes and fitted native head coordinates; it contains no replacement body or skeleton. Eye materials/UVs retain their source credit. The native athlete's hair and cap remain unchanged.

Full D/F characters remain available for the separate NPC inspection: independently colored casual shirts/shorts, fitted hair and nine authored civilian activities. F's source download retains its representative expressive face poses; the full CloudRig control interface stays in the original source. The native source downloads remain unchanged. The main race map is not replaced by the full-resolution NPC inspection sample.
