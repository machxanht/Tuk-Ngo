Full original body/skin provenance, source hashes and real Thanh Niên/Báo Sóc Trăng 2024 reference links remain in `../human/ATTRIBUTION.md` and `../coherent-v19/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Real press media inform the forms and stroke; they are not republished game textures. The actor is generic, not an identifiable athlete.

Houses and vehicles are authored project geometry. Existing bank material maps and daylight HDR retain their Poly Haven CC0 provenance. This folder contains no newly acquired third-party car/house model, Renderpeople model or Adobe/Mixamo model, and no AI-generated reference image. The accepted hull/daylight remain in `../coherent-v19/`.
