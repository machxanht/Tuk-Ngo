# Ghe Ngo hybrid V32

Stable tablet/game URL: https://machxanht.github.io/Tuk-Ngo/assets/illustrated-v22/

The canonical project root opens this same version. The shared visual direction is [game-direction.md](game-direction.md): bright painted festival banks, real 3D hull/athletes, fixed elevated bank view and right-to-left racing. Explicit legacy `demo` URLs remain references.

## Play and inspect

- **Chọn hai đội · mùa 2025**: choose from the eight men's quarter-finalists. The near lane is the player and the far lane the opponent. Hull/kit appearance changes; simulated strength stays the same. Selection locks during a player race.
- **Đua nhanh**: choose a difficulty, start, and tap Chèo when the marker enters the bright timing zone. Recovery/steady/sprint change cadence and endurance. The existing tested rules model runs a 1,200 m race with an independent AI, actual bow crossings, results and replay.
- **Giải 8 ghe**: quarterfinal, semifinal and final use the same race rules; wins advance, ties replay and losses eliminate. Completed heats persist locally. Opponent outcomes are seeded simulations, not a replay of historical rankings.
- **Đua tự động**: an approximately 46-second two-boat start-to-finish asset demonstration, with its own countdown and late sprint.
- **Tập chèo**: one-boat timing practice at a chosen cadence.
- **VĐV cận**: inspect seated/standing/caller/controller/helm motion, jersey colours and any point in the continuous cycle. This tool is hidden during a player race.

Stroke controls sit outside the picture, including in fullscreen. Short landscape screens place them beside the scene. Pause/resume freezes the player race; switching away from the page pauses it automatically. Winning records are local to this browser, with an in-memory fallback if storage is unavailable; they do not sync between devices. Optional licensed crowd ambience starts only after a sound-button gesture. Chế độ nhẹ lowers 3D resolution and the draw target to 20 fps.

## Assets and animation

Eight shared pose rigs animate 55 athletes per boat: eight seated pairs, seventeen standing pairs, caller, controller and three helmsmen. The preserved CC0 anatomical mesh has regenerated sewn vest/shorts, continuous bindings, fitted forward/backward caps, fingers and eyes. V27 restores its skin UVs and smooth seam normals, using the original licensed MakeHuman texture. The 97-sample authored clips interpolate continuously. Hands grip the paddle, feet sit on real internal supports and the hull occludes the legs. Twelve localized face profiles change jaw, cheeks, chin, nose and eye spacing, with hairlines/stubble and seeded crew distributions. They derive from one body, not independently sculpted or scanned identities. The inspector can show each variant's face clearly.

The source hull retains the accepted 3D shape. V28 uses the existing source-linked eight-team SVG kbach with original hull UVs and known shirt/cap/leadership palettes. Tailoring and several land-hull-to-race-bib matches remain approximate; see [team attribution](teams-2025/ATTRIBUTION.md). Runtime-fitted short sleeves follow original skin weights. The raw downloadable hull GLB retains its original source texture; in-game paint is applied by the renderer. The archived V25 manifest lists its original ten GLBs. The current runtime uses `athlete-rig-v27.glb` (4,230,492 bytes with embedded skin), retaining the same hull and eight role clips. `skin-v27-provenance.json` documents the source. The original V27 GLB remains a generic source. New V32 seated/standing/caller/controller downloads contain the currently chosen generic face, kit and role motion, with connected-shirt corrections in ordinary glTF morph animation. Hair/stubble uses vertex colour in the portable files, omitting fine shader grain. These are four sample roles; in-game kits cover all eight selected teams. The thin runtime collar line is omitted in portable files.

The scene contains 1,949 placed spectators, 64 walkers, 16 vehicles and ten stalls, plus two stands and one visible upstream bridge. These are authored placement counts. V32 brings the crowd to 28 looks and 64 painted frames: the existing 24 pairs plus four four-phase actions for overhead clapping, waving, photographing and a cheering child. Ages, outfits and actions mix by position rather than shirt-colour rows. Independent cheer windows and gentle continuous body motion keep fixed foot anchors. Alpha-component atlas metadata prevents neighbouring hands/shoes entering a cutout. Walking uses four painted steps; riders are painted into moving vehicle sprites. Water has drifting ripples/glints and paddle-contact droplets, denser at sprint cadence. Sprite limbs switch source poses; they are not continuously skinned crowd limbs.

The existing 61-entry 2025 registration list is reachable from this same game and returns here. Fifty-five entries have sourced Khmer pagoda names (45 press, 10 community directory); six are explicitly unverified. Registration ordinal is separate from race bib. These list entries are not 61 completed playable models. V29 also fixes Kos Thum's blue interior by identifying hull primitives after optimization removed their material names, while preserving the brown wooden supports.

V32 replaces sleeved tank overlays with one connected 533-vertex, 896-triangle shirt. It has four openings (neck, hem, two cuffs), no detached shoulder flap, team sleeve colours, a thin collar and `កូនខ្មែរ` printed on the back with the bundled Khmer font. The GPU skins original anatomical endpoints before interpolating cut vertices. Eight actual role rigs were tested at 17 phases in steady and sprint timing: maximum surface offset 5.518mm and packed-shader error below 0.045mm. This proves correspondence to the posed source surface; it is not a universal cloth-collision proof. Sleeveless team kits retain their vest. Four V32 portable role models and current gallery clips are linked from the game.

Approved raster sources, prompts, atlas anchors, hashes and optional PNG downloads remain in this folder. Four-pose rower PNGs are style references, not the live athlete animation. The art is original generated illustration, not evidence of historical kit or exact Maspéro architecture. Khmer banners use the bundled Noto Sans Khmer font; no national flags are present. Current event and welcome terminology was checked against Khmer reporting and official Cambodian pages, recorded in the V32 release notes. See [model/audio attribution](HYBRID-ATTRIBUTION.md) and [font attribution](FONT-ATTRIBUTION.md). Audio is a CC0 concert crowd recording, not Khmer chants or a tournament recording.

## Rebuild and checks

The source remains in the existing repository. `src/race/gameplay.mjs` supplies the same tested game rules as the legacy 3D version. `src/race/hybrid-boats-v25.mjs` supplies shared geometry/depth/animation; `preview.js`, `quick-race-v26.js`, `race-demo.js` and `festival-scene.js` compose the game and festival. Run `node scripts/build-preview-v26.mjs` after changing the game wrapper or hybrid renderer. After a geometry rebuild, `export-skin-hero-v27.py` and `build-skin-asset-v27.mjs` export/pack the textured UV-correct prototype; then rebuild the browser bundles. See `docs/hybrid-v27.md` in the repository.

Affected browser checks cover actual buttons, repeat-input prevention, pause, endurance, independent AI, fullscreen, replay, records after reload, win/DNF results, rig continuity/grip contact, frame changes and layouts. A real browser-clock automated-button race finished with 275 perfect strokes and a 3:10.60 player time; that is a test run, not a human playtest. Detailed current evidence is in `docs/hybrid-v26.md` in the repository. Physical tablet performance has not been measured.

The publisher updates this same folder and canonical root on the latest Pages tree, preserving legacy bundles and unrelated working changes. The agreed game scope combines the source-based eight-team selection, touch quick race, timing practice, three-round cup and automatic demo in one renderer. Historical tailoring/kbach and architecture are authored approximations with source limits; real-device performance remains unmeasured.
