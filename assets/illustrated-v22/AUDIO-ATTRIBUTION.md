# Crowd audio

`crowd-cheer-cc0.mp3` is the high-quality public MP3 preview of **RoaringCrowd.wav**, by **benfree**, published 5 October 2011 on Freesound.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/benfree/sounds/130568/
- Asset: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/130/130568_2024417-hq.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original description: rock concert roaring audience; duration 19.293 s.

This is a real audience recording used as game ambience. It is **not a recording of Khmer chants or of the Maspéro tournament**. No audio was extracted from the supplied YouTube broadcast. Playback requires the viewer's sound-button gesture; pausing the scene pauses the crowd audio.
