# Athlete model

The body, face, topology, skin weights, and humanoid/finger skeleton come from **Quaternius — Universal Base Characters, Superhero Male**, released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

- Original author and license: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- Official pack: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- Preserved pack license: `QUATERNIUS-LICENSE.txt`
- GLB repack: https://github.com/programasweights/avatar/blob/ddd5fc34a445bcded3cf9836607aaeebc19a5c78/public/assets/character.glb
- Repack provenance: https://github.com/programasweights/avatar/blob/ddd5fc34a445bcded3cf9836607aaeebc19a5c78/ASSETS.md

The downloaded GLB is preserved as `quaternius-athlete.glb`. The application clones the rig, adds uniform vertex colors, cap/pupil accessories, seated leg poses, finger closure and fixed-length arm IK. No generated sphere/cylinder body is used for the active crew. This is a generic licensed character, not a recreation of a named athlete.

The boat livery is derived from the graphic reference supplied by the project owner. It is a design variant, not asserted to be the original 2024 race livery. The river and droplet effects are realtime illustrations; cadence and speed are preview controls.
