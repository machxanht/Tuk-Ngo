# 2025 team GLBs and authored kbach

Eight game variants use the existing accepted owner-supplied hull shape, MakeHuman/MPFB CC0 athlete source and authored role animations. Human licensing is retained in `/Tuk-Ngo/assets/human/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Khmer shirt lettering is the owner's requested custom **កូនខ្មែរ**, not a copied historical sponsor print.

The SVG paint is newly authored vector drawing based on directly inspected land and race footage. No video/photo pixels are included. Kbach/hull matching is incomplete for several fleets; these are game reconstructions with recorded limits, not eight exact tournament replicas. See repository `docs/top-eight-2025-results.md`, `docs/top-eight-2025-appearance.md`, the two hull follow-up documents and `docs/crew-v13.md`. Later uploads of the 2025 race are distinguished from 2025 land uploads.
