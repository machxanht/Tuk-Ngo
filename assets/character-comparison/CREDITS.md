# D/F athlete comparison

Original D character: **Quaternius Universal Base Characters Standard**, Superhero Male Full Body, Simple Parted hair. CC0.

- https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original F character and shared shirt/pants geometry: **Snow Rig © Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org**, CC BY 4.0. Source: Snow v4.2, downloaded from the official Snow v4 page. The embedded `ReadMe.txt` links to CC BY 4.0.

- https://studio.blender.org/characters/snow/v4/
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptations for this comparison: compact body skeleton, original Snow facial poses baked to Smile/Effort/Blink morph targets, portable UDIM skin atlas, dark hair, a shared blue racing shirt, shorts cut from Snow pants, shirt fitting to D, removal of skin covered by clothing, shared race paddle and rowing cycle from Tuk Ngo. Clothing derived from Snow is credited on both D and F.

These are downloadable animated GLBs, not screenshots or AI-generated stand-ins. D has no added facial expressions. F includes a representative subset of the original rig's facial controls; it does not contain the full CloudRig control interface. The live race still uses its existing athlete assets.
